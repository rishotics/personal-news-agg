🕉 This Day Significance & History

Aja Ekadashi
Krishna Paksha Ekadashi, Bhadrapada month, Punarvasu nakshatra, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today falls the Krishna Paksha Ekadashi of Bhadrapada month, known across the tradition as Aja Ekadashi.

The story goes back to King Harishchandra of Ayodhya, a ruler so devoted to truth that he gave away his kingdom in fulfillment of a promise made in a dream.

Reduced to poverty, he was forced to sell his wife Shaivya and son Rohitashva, and he himself became a servant at the cremation grounds of a Chandala in Kashi, collecting fees for the last rites of the dead.

Even in this state of utter ruin he never once spoke a falsehood, holding fast to dharma though everything else had been stripped from him.

One day the sage Gautama, moved by his suffering, told him of this very Ekadashi and instructed him to fast and keep vigil through the night in devotion to Vishnu.

Harishchandra obeyed, and tradition says his sins fell away, his son was restored to life, and his kingdom and family were returned to him, so the day asks its observers to hold to truth and patience even when fortune seems to have abandoned them entirely.
