🤖 Paper of the Day AI & Robotics Research

LLM

Do Language Models Need a Trainable Input Embedding Table? Fixed Minimal Token Codes at 1.7B-Class Scale

Independent / academic · ['A. Bochkov']
Why it matters
  1. Tests whether trainable token embeddings, a universal assumption in LLM design, are actually necessary for strong performance.
  2. Replaces the learned embedding table with fixed, minimal token codes and still trains a competitive 1.7B-class language model.
  3. Challenges a foundational architectural default that virtually every transformer LLM has inherited without question since GPT-2.
  4. If confirmed at scale, this could cut parameter counts, memory footprint, and vocabulary-related engineering overhead across the industry.
  5. Directly relevant to anyone building or fine-tuning LLMs, since embedding tables often account for a large share of total parameters.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Masik Shivaratri (Pradosh Vrat)
Krishna Paksha Trayodashi (moving into Chaturdashi after 10:15 PM), Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon wanes toward darkness on Krishna Trayodashi, the eve that carries both the monthly Pradosh Vrat and Masik Shivaratri into the night.

Tradition recalls that long ago the Devas and Asuras churned the cosmic ocean together with Mount Mandara as their rod and the serpent Vasuki as their rope, searching for the nectar of immortality.

Before the nectar rose, a terrible poison called Halahala surged from the waters, and its fumes alone threatened to consume the three worlds.

Seeing every god and demon flee in terror, Lord Shiva stepped forward out of compassion and swallowed the poison to spare creation.

He held it fast in his throat so it would spread no further, and the poison's fire turned his neck blue, giving him the name Neelkanth.

In his honour devotees keep a fast through this night, offer water and bael leaves to the Shivalinga, and stay watchful till dawn, asking only to carry a little less poison and a little more restraint into their own lives.
