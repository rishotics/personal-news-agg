TokenGate: Adapting LLMs for Fast and Memory-Efficient Prefill with Learned Token Pruning

Stanford / UC Berkeley · Liana Patel, Rishabh Ranjan, Ion Stoica, Matei Zaharia, Carlos Guestrin
Why it matters
  1. Introduces TokenGate, a learned token-pruning method that skips unneeded tokens during prefill rather than processing every token through every layer.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Vijaya Dashami (Bijoya Dashami) – Durga Puja Immersion
Shukla Paksha Dashami (turning to Ekadashi by afternoon), Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon sits on Shukla Dashami of Ashwin, the tenth bright day that closes the nine nights of Navratri and Durga Puja.

Tradition holds that for nine nights Goddess Durga, mounted on her lion, fought the shape-shifting buffalo demon Mahishasura who had driven the gods from heaven.

On the tenth dawn she pinned him in his half-emerged buffalo form and struck off his head, ending his tyranny over the three worlds.

This triumph gives the day its name, Vijaya Dashami, the victorious tenth, and in Bengal it is kept as Bijoya Dashami, when married women perform sindoor khela, smearing vermillion on the goddess's image and on one another before the clay idols are lifted onto the shoulders of the crowd.

The images are then carried in procession to the river for visarjan, sending Durga back across the heavens to her husband Shiva on Mount Kailash with a promise that she will return the following autumn.

The day asks devotees to let go of the goddess without sorrow, carrying her victory over evil quietly into the ordinary days that follow.
