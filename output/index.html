🕉 This Day Significance & History

Ashwin Shukla Dashami — Bharat Milap
Shukla Paksha Dashami tithi (running until roughly 2:11 pm, after which Ekadashi begins) in the Hindu month of Ashwin, Vikram Samvat 2083.

Today's tithi, Ashwin Shukla Dashami, is the day right after Dussehra, and in Varanasi it is kept as Bharat Milap, the enactment of Rama's reunion with his brother Bharat after the fourteen-year exile, staged the day after Vijayadashami at the Nati Imli ground in Varanasi.

The tale begins when Queen Kaikeyi, egged on by her maid Manthara, demanded that Bharat be crowned king of Ayodhya and that Rama be exiled to the forest for fourteen years.

Bound by his father's word, Rama left for the forest with his wife Sita and brother Lakshmana, and King Dasharatha, devastated by his own promise, died of grief soon after.

When Bharat returned and learned what had happened, he rejected the throne his mother had won for him and set out with the people of Ayodhya to find Rama and beg him to come back and rule.

Rama refused to break his father's vow, so Bharat carried his brother's sandals back to Ayodhya, placed them on the throne, and governed only as a caretaker awaiting his brother's return.

The day asks those who observe it to hold up Bharat's example of placing loyalty and duty above personal gain, for he ruled Ayodhya by keeping Rama's wooden sandals on the throne rather than taking it for himself.
