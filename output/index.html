ETH Zurich · Amirhossein Kazemipour, Hehui Zheng, Robert Katzschmann
Why it matters
  1. Demonstrates a single anthropomorphic hand learning to walk on its own fingers while keeping its original manipulation design intact.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Bhadrapada Shukla Saptami
Shukla Paksha Saptami tithi (till 1:01 pm, then Ashtami begins), Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083, Jyeshtha Nakshatra, Friday

Today the moon is seven days into its waxing fortnight of Bhadrapada, the Saptami tithi that Hindu tradition sets aside for Surya, the sun god.

Elders tell that Surya crosses the sky each day in a resplendent chariot yoked to seven horses, the seven colours of light that make up the sun's single white ray.

His charioteer is Aruna, the ruddy dawn given form, who stands facing backward so that his own body can soften the sun's fierce brilliance before it reaches the world.

It is this partnership of light and shelter, tradition says, that lets crops ripen, eyes see, and life continue without being scorched by the very fire that sustains it.

On Saptami, devotees rise before sunrise, offer water and red flowers toward the rising sun, and recite hymns such as the Aditya Hridayam for health and clarity of mind.

The day asks those who observe it to greet the sun with gratitude, shed a little inner darkness, and draw fresh energy the way the earth does at every dawn.

On This Day in History
1810
In Santiago, an open town meeting known as a cabildo abierto accepted the resignation of Chile's Spanish governor and elected a local junta in his place.
It is remembered as the first step of Chile's independence movement and is still celebrated every year as Fiestas Patrias, the nation's founding holiday.
1931
Japanese Kwantung Army officers detonated explosives on a South Manchuria Railway line near Mukden and blamed Chinese soldiers, using the staged blast as a pretext to seize the city and invade Manchuria.
The false-flag operation exposed the League of Nations' weakness and set Japan on the path of military expansion that would help ignite the Second World War in Asia.
1947
The U.S. National Security Act took effect, merging the Army, Navy and the newly created Air Force into a single defense establishment.
The law laid the institutional foundation for the modern American national-security state, including the structures that oversee defense and intelligence to this day.
