🕉 This Day Significance & History

Rishi Panchami
Shukla Paksha Panchami (tithi at sunrise is Chaturthi), Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon waxes into the fifth tithi of Bhadrapada's bright fortnight, a day set aside not for a deity of the great pantheon but for the Saptarishi, the seven sages who first carried dharma into the world.

The tale told at this vrat comes from Vidarbha, where a learned Brahmin named Uttank lived with his devoted wife and a daughter who had been widowed young.

One night the daughter's body was found covered in worms, and her grief-stricken parents could find no cause for such suffering.

Uttank turned to his own tapasya and saw that in a previous life his daughter had entered the kitchen and touched sacred vessels while in her monthly impurity, and had neglected the Rishi Panchami vow besides.

On her father's counsel she bathed, fasted, and worshipped the seven sages with full devotion, and as the puja closed the affliction lifted and her health returned.

The day still asks the same of those who keep it: to pause, to seek forgiveness for faults committed unknowingly, and to honour the sages through fasting and prayer.
