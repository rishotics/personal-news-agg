🕉 This Day Significance & History

Sharad Purnima (Kojagiri Purnima)
Shukla Paksha Chaturdashi till 11:56 AM, then Purnima begins, in the Hindu month of Ashwin (Ashwina), Vikram Samvat 2083.

Tonight the autumn moon of Ashwin swells to its fullest and clearest point of the year, its light said to carry a trace of amrita, the nectar of immortality.

Tradition holds that on this one night Goddess Lakshmi leaves her celestial seat and walks the earth, moving from household to household under the open sky.

She is said to ask, Ko Jagarti, meaning who is awake, and it is for this question that the night is called Kojagiri.

Those she finds alert, honest, and prepared, rather than careless or asleep, are the ones she chooses to bless with prosperity and good fortune.

Families keep vigil through the night, setting out kheer in the moonlight to soak in its rays before sharing it as prasad, while homes are lit with lamps and swept clean to welcome the goddess in.

The day asks its observers to stay wakeful in body and in conscience, since abundance, tradition says, favours those who are ready to receive it rather than those who merely wait for it.
