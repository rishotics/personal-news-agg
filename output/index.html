🕉 This Day Significance & History

Budha Pradosh Vrat
Krishna Paksha Dwadashi (turning to Trayodashi after nightfall), Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083.

Today the moon moves through Krishna Paksha Dwadashi of Ashwin and slides into Trayodashi after dark, the tithi Shiva devotees keep as Pradosh Vrat.

Tradition says the Moon god Chandra married all twenty-seven daughters of Daksha Prajapati but gave his whole heart only to Rohini, leaving the rest neglected.

The spurned wives complained to their father, and when Chandra ignored Daksha's warning to treat them equally, the enraged sage cursed him with a wasting disease.

As Chandra withered and the world itself began to dim in dread, the gods ran to Brahma for help, and he told Chandra to worship Shiva in the form of a lingam.

Chandra performed six months of penance until Shiva appeared before him as a Jyotirlinga, and though the curse could not be erased entirely, the Lord softened it into the moon's endless waxing and waning.

Because that mercy was granted at twilight on Trayodashi, dusk on this tithi is still kept as sacred time, and the day asks devotees to fast and pray to Shiva at sunset for release from suffering and old burdens.
