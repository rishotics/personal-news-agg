🕉 This Day Significance & History

Kalashtami (Krishna Paksha Ashtami)
Ashwin month, Krishna Paksha, Saptami tithi at sunrise turning to Ashtami by morning, Vikram Samvat 2083, Saturday.

Today's Krishna Paksha Ashtami in the month of Ashwin brings Kalashtami, the monthly day set aside for Lord Bhairava, the fierce form of Shiva.

Tradition tells that Brahma and Vishnu once argued over who among the gods was supreme, and Brahma's dishonest boast of victory provoked Shiva's anger.

From that anger Shiva brought forth Bhairava, who with a single stroke severed one of Brahma's five heads for his arrogance.

The skull stuck fast to Bhairava's palm as the sin of brahminicide, and he was forced to wander the three worlds as a penitent beggar until the sin finally left him on reaching the holy city of Varanasi.

Devotees mark the day with fasting, worship of Shiva and Parvati, and reverence for Bhairava's chosen companion, the dog, who is fed as an act of devotion.

The day asks worshippers to set aside ego, pride, and false claims to superiority, following Brahma's own hard lesson in humility.
