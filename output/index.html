🕉 This Day Significance & History

Karwa Chauth
Krishna Paksha Chaturthi, Kartika month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today falls on Krishna Paksha Chaturthi of the Hindu month of Kartik, the day married women across North India observe Karwa Chauth.

Tradition traces the fast to the tale of Queen Veervati, who was the only sister of seven devoted brothers and had married a king.

On her first Karwa Chauth after the wedding she kept a strict fast at her parents' home, but as the day wore on she grew faint with hunger and thirst, so her brothers placed a mirror in a peepal tree to trick her into thinking the moon had risen.

Believing her fast complete, Veervati ate, and almost at once came word that her husband had died.

Grief-stricken, she met Goddess Parvati on the road, who revealed the deception and told her that only a fast kept with full honesty until the true moonrise could undo the harm.

Veervati observed the vrat again the following year with complete devotion, her husband was restored to life, and ever since the day asks married women to fast from sunrise to moonrise for their husband's long life, breaking the fast only after sighting the moon through a sieve.
