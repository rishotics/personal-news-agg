🕉 This Day Significance & History

Sankashti Chaturthi
Krishna Paksha Chaturthi, Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083 (tithi runs until roughly 2:55 PM, after which Panchami begins)

Today the moon wanes into Krishna Paksha Chaturthi of the Ashwin month, a tithi that tradition sets aside every month for Lord Ganesha.

The story behind this belongs to how Ganesha came to be, as told across Puranic tradition: Parvati, wanting a guard while she bathed, shaped a boy out of turmeric paste and breathed life into him, setting him at her door.

When Shiva returned home and found this unfamiliar boy blocking his own entrance, an argument turned violent and Shiva struck off the boy's head, not realizing he was his own son.

Parvati's grief was so great that Shiva sent his attendants out to bring back the head of the first living creature they found, which turned out to be an elephant, and he placed it on the boy's shoulders to restore him to life.

Reborn as Ganesha, the boy was named remover of obstacles and given the boon that he would be invoked before every undertaking and remembered on every Chaturthi that follows.

Because this particular Chaturthi falls in the waning fortnight, it is kept as Sankashti Chaturthi, and devotees fast through the day, break the vrat only after sighting the moon at night, and ask Ganesha to clear the hurdles standing in their way.
