🤖 Paper of the Day AI & Robotics Research

LLM

Amplified Does Not Mean Predictive: Reasoning Behaviors in Thinking Models

CMU · Jean de Dieu Nyandwi, Leena Mathur, Yonatan Bisk, Robert Hawkins, Graham Neubig
Why it matters
  1. Investigates whether reasoning-oriented RL training actually amplifies the behaviors most correlated with correct answers in thinking models
  2. Finds a key dissociation: reasoning traces become longer and more elaborate without those added behaviors being predictive of correctness
  3. Directly challenges the widely assumed causal story behind chain-of-thought scaling and reasoning-model post-training pipelines
  4. Affects every lab building 'thinking' or reasoning-RL models, since it questions what current training objectives are actually optimizing
  5. Suggests future work must separate surface-level deliberation from behaviors causally tied to accuracy, reshaping reasoning-model evaluation and training design
