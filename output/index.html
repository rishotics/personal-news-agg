🕉 This Day Significance & History

Krishna Paksha Shashthi / Saptami Shraddh (Pitru Paksha)
Krishna Paksha Shashthi until 10:15 AM (then Saptami begins), Ashwin month (Purnimanta) / Bhadrapada (Amanta), Vikram Samvat 2083, within Pitru Paksha.

Today's tithi falls inside Pitru Paksha, the dark fortnight set aside each year for remembering one's ancestors.

Tradition traces the custom to Karna, the great warrior of the Mahabharata, who after dying in battle ascended to the heavens expecting rest.

There he found that whatever food he touched turned instantly to gold, and hunger gnawed at him even in paradise.

When he asked the reason, he was told that in life he had given away wealth and gold generously but had never once offered food or water to his own ancestors, for he had grown up not knowing his true lineage.

Moved by his plight, he was sent back to earth for a fixed number of days to perform shraddha, feeding the hungry and pouring water in memory of his forebears before he could finally eat in peace.

The day asks the living to pause, remember those who came before them, and offer food, water, or charity in their name so that the debt of gratitude is not left unpaid.
