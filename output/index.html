🕉 This Day Significance & History

Onam (Thiruvonam) — Shravana Shukla Trayodashi
Shukla Paksha Trayodashi, Shravana month, Vikram Samvat 2083 (Wednesday, Shravana nakshatra)

Today falls on Shukla Paksha Trayodashi of the Shravana month, and in Kerala it is Thiruvonam, the central day of the ten-day Onam festival.

Tradition holds that the Asura king Mahabali once ruled the earth so justly that his kingdom knew no poverty, injustice, or falsehood, and his people wanted for nothing.

Threatened by his growing power, the gods approached Lord Vishnu, who descended as the dwarf Brahmin Vamana and asked the ever-generous king for only as much land as could be covered in three steps.

Mahabali agreed despite his guru's warning, and Vamana then grew into the cosmic Trivikrama, covering the earth with one stride and the heavens with the next.

With no ground left for the third step, Mahabali bowed and offered his own head, and the pleased Vishnu pressed him gently down to the netherworld while granting him one wish, to return each year and see his people again.

Onam marks that promised homecoming, and the day asks people to welcome Mahabali with the same humility, honesty, and generosity that made his reign a golden age worth remembering.
