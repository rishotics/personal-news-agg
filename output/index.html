🕉 This Day Significance & History

Shravana Shukla Purnima – Satyanarayan Vrat
Shukla Paksha Chaturdashi tithi till mid-morning, then Purnima begins; Shravana month, Vikram Samvat 2083, Nakshatra Shravana turning to Dhanishta.

Troubled by the suffering he witnessed among mortals during his travels, the sage Narada went before Lord Vishnu and begged for a remedy that any person could follow.

Vishnu answered by describing the Satyanarayan Vrat, a puja of fruit, milk, ghee and wheat that can be observed by anyone, regardless of caste or station, on a full-moon day.

To prove its power he recalled a poor Brahmin in Kashi whom he approached in the disguise of an old man and told to perform the puja, after which the Brahmin's poverty lifted.

A woodcutter who vowed to perform the same puja with his day's earnings likewise found his fortunes doubled, and a merchant named Sadhu grew wealthy and was blessed with a daughter after keeping the vow.

Sadhu later forgot his promise once prosperity returned, and only after his ships were lost and his family plunged into hardship did his wife's devotion move him to complete the puja and have everything restored.

The day therefore calls on households to gather kin and neighbours for the puja, to honour vows made in both want and plenty, and to share the sanctified prasad without pride.

On This Day in History
1883
The volcano Krakatoa in Indonesia erupted in one of history's most violent eruptions.
The blast and the tsunamis it triggered killed tens of thousands and reshaped scientific understanding of global climate shocks from volcanic events.
1928
Fifteen nations signed the Kellogg-Briand Pact in Paris, formally renouncing war as an instrument of national policy.
Though it failed to prevent World War II, the pact's language later underpinned the Nuremberg prosecutions and remains a foundation of modern international law against aggression.
1991
Moldova declared independence from the Soviet Union as the USSR was breaking apart.
It marked one of the final dominoes in the collapse of the Soviet bloc, reshaping the map of Eastern Europe and creating a state still contested by Russian influence today.
