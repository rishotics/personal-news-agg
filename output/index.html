WHALE: A Simple Recipe for Joint Harness-Weight Optimization

Stanford / academic collaboration · ['Haechan Kim', 'Yoonho Lee', 'Gisang Lee', 'Chelsea Finn', 'Kangwook Lee']
Why it matters
  1. Proposes jointly optimizing an agent's model weights and its executable harness code instead of tuning either alone.
  2. Novelty is treating harness and weights as co-adapting variables, since fixing one can bottleneck gains from the other.
  3. Backed by Chelsea Finn and collaborators, giving the approach credibility from a leading agent-learning research group.
  4. Affects anyone building LLM agent systems, since most production agents rely on hand-tuned scaffolding around frozen models.
  5. Suggests future agent development pipelines should co-design harness and weights rather than optimizing them in separate stages.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Krishna Janmashtami
Ashtami tithi, Krishna Paksha, Bhadrapada month (Rohini nakshatra)

Today falls on Krishna Ashtami of Krishna Paksha in the month of Bhadrapada, the tithi tradition sets aside as Krishna Janmashtami, the birthday of Lord Krishna.

The legend tells that Krishna was born at midnight in a dark prison cell in Mathura, the eighth child of Devaki and Vasudeva.

His maternal uncle, the tyrant king Kansa, had already killed Devaki's first seven children after a prophecy warned that her eighth son would end his reign.

As Krishna was born the prison chains are said to have loosened and the guards fell into a deep sleep, so Vasudeva carried the infant through a raging storm and across the swollen Yamuna river to the safety of Gokul.

There he exchanged the baby for the newborn daughter of Nanda and Yashoda, and Krishna grew up among the cowherds of Vrindavan before one day returning to Mathura to fulfil the prophecy and slay Kansa.

The day asks devotees to keep a fast until midnight, rock the cradle of the infant Krishna, and renew their trust that righteousness finds a way even when it seems most hemmed in by tyranny.
