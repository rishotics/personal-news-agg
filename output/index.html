🕉 This Day Significance & History

Sharad Navratri Begins (Ghatasthapana)
Shukla Paksha Pratipada, Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today marks Ghatasthapana, the ceremonial start of Sharad Navratri, when a sacred kalash is installed to invoke the Goddess for nine nights of worship.

The story behind the festival goes back to the demon Mahishasura, who won a boon of invincibility and then drove the gods out of heaven.

Unable to defeat him alone, Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva poured their combined radiance into one point, and from that fused light Goddess Durga was born.

Armed with weapons given by each god and riding the lion gifted by Himavan, Durga fought Mahishasura for nine days as he shifted between buffalo, man, lion and elephant to escape her.

On the tenth day, when he returned to his buffalo form, she pinned him down and drove her trident through his heart, restoring dharma and freeing the three worlds.

The day asks devotees to install the kalash with devotion, begin their nine-day fasts and prayers, and look inward at the pride and fear that Mahishasura is said to represent.
