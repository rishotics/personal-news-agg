🕉 This Day Significance & History

Bhadrapada Purnima
Shukla Paksha Purnima (waxing fifteenth), Purva Bhadrapada nakshatra, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon stands full and the tithi is Purnima, the day tradition sets aside for Lord Satyanarayana, the truth-form of Vishnu, and for Chandra, the moon god himself.

The Satyanarayana Katha recited on this day is a chain of tales woven around a poor brahmin, a humble woodcutter, a childless merchant, and even a proud king, each touched by the same god in disguise.

The merchant Sadhu Vaishya vows that if he is blessed with a child he will hold this puja, and when his daughter Kalavati is born he lets the promise slip from his mind for years.

Because the vow is forgotten, hardship follows the family, and it is only when Kalavati and her mother finally perform the vrat with sincere hearts that their fortunes turn and truth comes to light.

In another strand of the same story King Tungadhwaj returns from a hunt, sees cowherds worshipping Satyanarayana, and in his pride refuses the offered prasad, inviting loss upon his house until he humbly returns to accept it.

The day therefore asks every household, rich or poor, ruler or commoner, to keep its word once given and to receive whatever grace comes, however small, with gratitude rather than pride.
