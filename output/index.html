🤖 Paper of the Day AI & Robotics Research

LLM

β-OPSD: Deriving with Policy Optimization, Training with Self-Distillation

University of Maryland · Jiawei Xu, Minghui Liu, Juzheng Zhang, Tom Goldstein, Furong Huang
Why it matters
  1. Fixes a well-known brittleness in on-policy self-distillation, a leading method for improving reasoning LLMs via RL-free training
  2. Shows vanilla OPSD is just the beta=1 special case of a broader family, giving a principled knob to tune stability versus performance
  3. Reframes derivation as policy optimization while keeping training as self-distillation, unifying two previously separate views of the algorithm
  4. Matters directly to teams post-training reasoning models like o1-style or DeepSeek-R1 style systems that rely on self-distillation over RL
  5. Offers a concrete recipe to reduce the heavy engineering tuning currently required to make self-distillation reliable in production pipelines
