Virtual Head Attention

Baidu · Xibo Ding, Guoxia Wang, Jinle Zeng, Jiabin Yang, Sirui Tao
Why it matters
  1. Tackles the long-standing impossible triangle among KV cache size, training and prefill FLOPs, and model quality in long-context LLMs
  2. Introduces virtual attention heads that decouple cache compression from quality loss, unlike GQA, MLA, or MFA which trade off one against the other
  3. Positions itself directly against established production techniques like MLA and MFA used in major deployed models
  4. Matters for anyone serving long-context LLMs at scale since KV cache size directly drives memory cost and serving throughput
  5. Could reshape default attention architecture choices for next-generation long-context models if the triangle tradeoff is genuinely broken

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Durga Ashtami (Maha Ashtami)
Shukla Paksha Ashtami tithi (till approx. 10:52 AM, then Navami begins), Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today's panchang reads Ashtami tithi of Shukla Paksha in the month of Ashwin, the day the whole country knows as Durga Ashtami or Maha Ashtami, the eighth and most sacred day of Durga Puja.

On this day the Goddess is worshipped in her form as Mahagauri, said to represent the peak of her transformative power after the long churning of Navratri.

Tradition tells that when the demon generals Chanda and Munda crept up to ambush her in violation of the rules of war, Durga's face darkened with rage and the fierce Chamunda sprang out from her third eye to destroy them.

The precise crossing point between the Ashtami and Navami tithis is called Sandhi Puja, worship offered in the last twenty-four minutes of one tithi and the first twenty-four of the next, marking the very moment the slaying is believed to have occurred.

In many homes and pandals this day also brings Astra Puja, the ceremonial worship of weapons, remembering the arms Durga once wielded against the buffalo-demon Mahishasura.

The day asks devotees to meet cruelty and deceit without flinching, to offer flowers, fasting and prayer to the Mother, and to trust that righteous anger, rightly aimed, restores balance to the world.
