🕉 This Day Significance & History

Ganesh Chaturthi (Bhadrapada Shukla Chaturthi)
Bhadrapada month, Shukla Paksha; Tritiya tithi at sunrise giving way to Chaturthi around 7 AM, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon moves from Tritiya into Chaturthi of the bright fortnight of Bhadrapada, the tithi on which tradition places the birth of Lord Ganesha.

The story is told that Goddess Parvati, wishing for a guard while she bathed, shaped a boy out of turmeric paste and breathed life into him.

When Lord Shiva returned and found this unfamiliar boy barring his way to his own wife, a quarrel broke out and in his fury Shiva severed the boy's head.

Seeing Parvati's grief, Shiva sent his attendants out to bring back the head of the first living being they found, and an elephant's head was placed upon the boy to restore him to life.

Thus was Ganesha reborn, and the gods declared that he would be worshipped first before any undertaking, remover of obstacles and lord of beginnings.

The day asks devotees to install a clay image of Ganesha, offer him modak and durva grass, and seek his blessing before starting whatever comes next.
