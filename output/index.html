🕉 This Day Significance & History

Shravana Purnima – Raksha Bandhan
Shukla Paksha Purnima tithi in the month of Shravana, Vikram Samvat 2083, under Shatabhisha nakshatra

Today the moon stands full, marking Purnima at the close of the bright fortnight of Shravana, and this particular full moon is kept across India as Raksha Bandhan.

Tradition recalls the day queen Draupadi tore a strip of cloth from her sari and bound it around Krishna's finger to stop it bleeding after he had cut himself with his discus.

Touched by her care, Krishna is said to have promised that he would repay the debt of that thread whenever she needed him, a vow he kept years later when he came to her aid in the Kaurava court.

In memory of that bond, sisters today tie a rakhi, a sacred thread, around their brothers' wrists as a pledge of protection and affection.

Brothers in turn vow to shield their sisters from harm and often mark the moment with a gift.

The day asks people to renew such ties of care and protection, whether by blood or by choice, on the day the moon itself is whole.
