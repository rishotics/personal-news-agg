🕉 This Day Significance & History

Hala Shashthi (Balarama Jayanti)
Krishna Paksha Shashthi, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083 (Wednesday)

Today falls on Bhadrapada Krishna Shashthi, observed across North India as Hala Shashthi, sacred to Lord Balarama, the elder brother of Krishna.

Tradition holds that Balarama was conceived in Devaki's womb but mystically transferred into Rohini's to shield him from the tyrant king Kamsa, which is why he is also called Sankarshana.

He grew up wielding not a sword but a plough, the hala, earning him the name Haladhara and marking him as a guardian of the soil and of households.

The best-loved katha of this vrat tells of a pregnant milkmaid who, defying the fast, sold milk from a field ploughed that very day and gave birth alone beneath a jharberi tree, only for her newborn to die.

Realising her fault, she confessed before the women keeping the Hala Shashthi vrat and begged their forgiveness, and by their grace and Balarama's mercy her child was restored to life.

So today mothers fast from anything grown by the plough and pray to Balarama for the long life, health, and protection of their children.
