🤖 Paper of the Day AI & Robotics Research

LLM

Your Transformer Can Hold Two Thoughts at Once: Evidence of Linear Superposition in LLMs

Independent/Academic (mechanistic interpretability collective) · Pavel Tikhonov, Anton Korznikov, Matvey Mikhalchuk, Nikita Dragunov, Temurbek Rahmatullaev
Why it matters
  1. Shows that linearly combining inputs from two distinct text streams makes LLMs output a literal superposition of both next-token distributions.
  2. This challenges the assumption that transformer nonlinearity prevents simple linear algebra from predicting model behavior at the distribution level.
  3. The finding is dubbed the Superposition phenomenon and is demonstrated as a consistent, measurable effect rather than an occasional curiosity.
  4. Matters directly to interpretability researchers at Anthropic, DeepMind, and academia building linear-representation and superposition theories of LLM internals.
  5. Could reshape how practitioners think about prompt mixing, batching side effects, and safety analyses that assume independent processing of separate inputs.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Krishna Paksha Pratipada – Pitru Paksha Begins
Krishna Paksha Pratipada tithi, Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083, Sunday

Today the moon enters its waning fortnight, and with Krishna Paksha Pratipada the sixteen-day observance of Pitru Paksha opens in the Hindu calendar.

Tradition traces its origin to the Mahabharata, where the great warrior Karna, son of Surya, died on the battlefield of Kurukshetra and ascended to heaven expecting reward for his famous generosity.

There he was offered only gold and jewels to eat, and when he asked why, Indra told him that though Karna had donated wealth all his life, he had never once offered food or water to his own ancestors.

Karna protested that he had never known his true lineage, having been abandoned at birth by his mother Kunti, so he could not have honoured forebears he did not know existed.

Moved by this, Indra granted him leave to return to earth for a fixed number of days so he could perform shraddha and tarpan, feeding the hungry and offering water to his forefathers before going back to heaven satisfied.

The day asks the living to remember that lineage is a debt as much as a gift, and to mark it with food given to the needy, water offered in memory of the departed, and quiet gratitude toward those who came before.
