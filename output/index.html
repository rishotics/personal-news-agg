🕉 This Day Significance & History

Radha Ashtami
Shukla Paksha Ashtami, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today falls on Shukla Ashtami of Bhadrapada, and it is celebrated across Krishna-worshipping communities as Radha Ashtami, the appearance day of Radha Rani.

Tradition holds that she was born to Vrishabhanu and his wife Kirtida in Raval near Mathura, arriving not through an ordinary birth but as a divine infant discovered by her father.

One well-loved account from the Padma Purana tells that King Vrishabhanu, while clearing ground for a yagya, came upon the child resting on a lotus, and took her home as his own.

She is revered as an incarnation of Goddess Lakshmi and grew up in Barsana, becoming the eternal beloved of Krishna and the leader of the gopis of Vrindavan.

Where Janmashtami marks the arrival of God himself, Radha Ashtami is kept fifteen days later to honour the path of devotion through which the divine is reached, for it is said that worship of Krishna is incomplete without reverence for Radha.

The day asks devotees to rise early, bathe her image, offer fasting and prayer, and to remember that selfless, unconditional love is itself a form of worship.
