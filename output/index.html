🕉 This Day Significance & History

Goga Navami (Gugga Naumi)
Krishna Paksha Navami, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today falls on the Navami, the ninth day of the waning moon in Bhadrapada, a tithi that North India dedicates to Gogaji, the folk hero worshipped as lord of serpents.

Legend holds that a queen named Bachal, after long penance before the yogi Gorakhnath, was blessed with a son who carried power over snakes even from the womb.

Gogaji grew into a Rajput prince and warrior who could tame the deadliest serpents and was said to have humbled even Vasuki, the serpent king, by refusing to let his people be harmed.

He is remembered riding a blue horse under blue and yellow flags, defending his land and cattle, until a bitter feud within his own family and his final vanishing into the earth closed his earthly story.

In Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab and Himachal Pradesh, devotees gather at Gogaji's shrines today, carrying his image in procession and singing the ballads that keep his memory alive.

The day asks worshippers to seek his protection from snakebite and misfortune, with mothers in particular praying at his altar for the safety and long life of their children.
