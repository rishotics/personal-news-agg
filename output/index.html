🕉 This Day Significance & History

Krishna Paksha Navami (Ashwin) — Jivitputrika (Jitiya) Vrat Parana
Krishna Navami, Krishna Paksha, Ashwin month (Punarvasu nakshatra)

Today the Hindu calendar reads Krishna Navami of the dark fortnight in the month of Ashwin, the ninth lunar day after the full moon.

This is the closing day of Jivitputrika or Jitiya Vrat, the three-day fast mothers keep in Bihar, Jharkhand, and the wider Purvanchal belt for the long life of their children.

Yesterday, on Ashtami, mothers sat through a nirjala vrat, a full day and night without even a sip of water, and today at dawn they finally break that fast once the Ashtami tithi gives way to Navami.

The vrat's central tale is of King Jimutavahana, who is said to have offered his own body to the eagle-king Garuda so that a young serpent marked for death could live in his place.

A second story told alongside it, of an eagle and a jackal who both attempted the same fast, warns that only sincere devotion carries the vrat's power, not its mere performance.

The day asks mothers who observed the fast to complete it with that same steadfastness, taking their first water and food only after the proper hour, and to pass the story of Jimutavahana's sacrifice on to the children for whose sake it was kept.
