🕉 This Day Significance & History

Bhadrapada Shukla Dashami — Teja Dashami
Shukla Paksha Dashami (turning to Ekadashi around 8:00 PM), Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083, Monday, nakshatra Purva/Uttara Ashadha.

Today is Bhadrapada Shukla Dashami, the day tradition sets aside for the Rajasthani folk-hero Veer Tejaji, and it is kept across Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and parts of Haryana as Teja Dashami.

Tejaji was born to the Jat chieftain Tahad Dev of Kharnal village, and grew into a warrior famed for defending cattle and keeping his word at any cost.

The best-known tale tells how he rode out to recover cows carried off by raiders, and after a fierce fight to reclaim the herd, was gravely wounded.

On his way he had once injured a snake, Basak Naga, and had promised to let it bite him in return; true to his vow he offered the serpent his own tongue since no other part of his body was left unwounded, and the snake's bite finally took his life.

Because he chose death over breaking a promise, tradition holds that Basak Naga granted him status as a deity, and he is worshipped ever after as a guardian against snakebite and protector of cattle.

The day asks devotees to bathe at dawn, rest their bullocks and cows rather than yoke them to the plough, visit a Tejaji shrine with offerings of milk and churma, and recall in his story that truth and a given word are worth more than life itself.
