🕉 This Day Significance & History

Ananta Chaturdashi (Shukla Paksha Chaturdashi, Bhadrapada)
Shukla Paksha Chaturdashi tithi (until 11:06 PM, then Purnima begins), Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083, Friday, Shatabhisha Nakshatra

Today is Ananta Chaturdashi, the fourteenth day of the bright fortnight of Bhadrapada, dedicated to the boundless, infinite form of Lord Vishnu known as Ananta.

The vrat's best-known katha tells of the sage Kaundinya and his wife Sushila, who learns of the observance from a group of women by a riverbank and ties upon her arm the sacred fourteen-knotted thread called the Ananta Sutra.

When Kaundinya later notices the thread and, doubting its power, tears it off and throws it into the fire, misfortune swiftly falls upon his household and he loses his wealth and comfort.

Repentant and desperate, he wanders in search of the very tree from which the sacred thread first came, enduring hunger, exhaustion, and humility along the way.

Lord Vishnu himself eventually appears to him, forgives his doubt, and restores his fortune once his faith is proven sincere.

The day asks devotees to tie the Ananta Sutra, worship Vishnu's limitless form, and trust that what seems broken can be restored through patience and devotion, even as many also immerse their Ganesha idols on this same date to close the ten-day festival.
