🕉 This Day Significance & History

Krishna Paksha Dashami
Dashami tithi, Krishna Paksha, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083 (Ardra nakshatra)

Today the moon stands on Krishna Paksha Dashami, the tenth day of the waning fortnight in the month of Bhadrapada, Vikram Samvat 2083.

Tradition assigns each lunar day its own presiding force, and Dashami belongs to Yamaraja, the lord of dharma who judges every soul's deeds.

The Padma Purana tells how Yamaraja himself came into being at the start of creation to hold the sin-demon Papapurusha in check, sending the guilty to their reckoning in the hereafter.

It was to overpower that very Papapurusha that Ekadashi, the day sacred to Vishnu, was born soon after, which is why the day just before her, this Dashami, is kept as the threshold of preparation.

Devout households honour it quietly by taking a single simple meal in the evening, emptying the body of grain so it stands clean for the fast that begins at sunrise tomorrow.

The day asks nothing dramatic of anyone, only that old debts be settled, promises kept, and the heart made honest before crossing into Ekadashi.
