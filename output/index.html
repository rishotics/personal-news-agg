🕉 This Day Significance & History

Bhaum Pradosh Vrat
Krishna Paksha Trayodashi, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083 — falling on a Tuesday

Today the moon stands on Krishna Paksha Trayodashi in the month of Bhadrapada, and because the thirteenth tithi has landed on a Tuesday it is kept as Bhaum Pradosh Vrat.

Pradosh itself is Shiva's own twilight, the roughly ninety-minute window around sunset when the god is believed to be worshipped with special grace, and the thirteenth tithi of every fortnight is set aside for him.

Tuesday belongs to Mangal, the planet Mars, so tradition holds that Shiva's evening tithi joins with Mars's day and the fast takes the name Bhaum, or Bhauma, Pradosh.

Householders who keep this vrat turn to it chiefly for release from debt and from the harsher effects of Mars, whether that shows up as conflict, illness, or simple restlessness, and many bow to Hanuman too since Tuesday is his day as well.

The evening is kept simply, with a day-long fast, a visit to Nandi before the Shivalinga, and prayers to Shiva and Parvati as dusk falls, the Pradosh katha often read aloud for the whole household to hear.

The day asks only that the fast be broken after the sunset puja with a little water and plain prasad, so that courage and calm settle in before the light goes.
