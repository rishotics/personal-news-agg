RoboJEPA: Scaling Robotic Latent World Models

Meta (FAIR) / collaborators · Artem Zholus, Nicolas Beltran-Velez, Jianhao Yuan, Sarath Chandar, Tushar Nagarajan
Why it matters
  1. Studies how JEPA-style latent world models for robotics scale with model size, data volume, and compute budget, filling a gap in the field.
  2. Novelty lies in providing the first systematic scaling-law analysis for robotic latent world models rather than another one-off architecture paper.
  3. Credibility comes from controlled experiments isolating scaling axes to predict downstream planning and prediction performance before expensive training runs.
  4. Affects anyone building robot foundation models, since compute allocation decisions currently rely on intuition rather than empirical scaling curves.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Krishna Paksha Chaturdashi (Chaturdashi Shraddh, Pitru Paksha)
Krishna Paksha Chaturdashi tithi (till 9:35-9:36 PM, then Amavasya begins), Ashwina month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon wanes to its fourteenth night, the Krishna Chaturdashi of Ashwin, and the almanac marks it as Chaturdashi Shraddh, the second-to-last day of Pitru Paksha.

Tradition holds that for fifteen days each year the pitris, the departed ancestors, descend from their realm to receive what their children offer in water, rice, and sesame.

Elders say that while most souls are remembered on the tithi of their death, this particular chaturdashi is set aside for those who met an untimely end, by accident, weapon, or a death that came before its time.

Families who are uncertain of the exact day a forebear passed, or who lost someone to sudden misfortune, perform the shraddha today so that the soul is not left without its due.

Tomorrow the fortnight closes with Sarvapitri Amavasya, the no-moon night on which every ancestor, named or forgotten, is honored at once.

The day asks the living to pause, feed a crow or a cow if one can be found, and offer the rite with a quiet mind, so that old grief between the living and the dead may finally settle.
