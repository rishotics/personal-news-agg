🕉 This Day Significance & History

Lalita Panchami (Upang Lalita Vrat)
Panchami tithi, Shukla Paksha, Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083 — the fifth day of Sharad Navratri

Today falls on Ashwin Shukla Panchami, and the panchang for this Thursday reads Panchami tithi, Shukla Paksha, Jyeshtha nakshatra in the month of Ashwin.

This is the fifth day of Sharad Navratri, observed across India as Lalita Panchami, a day dedicated to Goddess Lalita Tripura Sundari, a divine manifestation of Shakti who symbolizes grace and supreme power.

She is counted among the ten Mahavidyas and is also known by the names Shodashi and Tripura Sundari, an exalted form of the Goddess Durga herself.

Tradition tells that the demon Bhandasura, born from the ashes of Kamadeva, grew so tyrannical in his ignorance and ego that he threatened the balance of the three worlds, and Goddess Lalita emerged in her magnificent form, armed with celestial weapons, to vanquish him and restore cosmic order.

In households across Maharashtra and Gujarat especially, women keep a fast this day and recite the Lalita Sahasranama, inviting the goddess's thousand names into the home.

The day asks devotees to set aside the ego and ignorance that Bhandasura represented, and to seek instead the goddess's clarity, courage and inner balance.
