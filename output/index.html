IntBMoE: Integrating Block-Level Conditioning into Expert Composition for Full-Participation Mixture-of-Experts

Alibaba · Ran Cheng, Longfei Xu, Zheng Liu, Kaikui Liu, Xiangxiang Chu
Why it matters
  1. Introduces IntBMoE, a Mixture-of-Experts design that decouples participation, execution, and materialization instead of tying them together as in standard MoE routing
  2. Enables full-participation MoE where every expert can influence a token's output without forcing full compute or memory materialization for each token
  3. Addresses a core scaling bottleneck in production LLMs where MoE capacity gains are usually bought with wasted compute or degraded routing quality
  4. Comes from Alibaba, a lab actively shipping large MoE models like Qwen, making this directly relevant to next-generation model architectures
  5. Signals a shift in how future frontier MoE systems could be engineered, letting capacity, cost, and memory footprint be tuned independently

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Parsva (Parivartini) Ekadashi
Shukla Paksha Ekadashi, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon stands on the eleventh day of the bright fortnight of Bhadrapada, a tithi tradition calls Parsva or Parivartini Ekadashi, sacred to Lord Vishnu in his Vamana form.

The story goes that the generous demon-king Mahabali, grandson of Prahlad, had grown so powerful through his devotion and charity that he had pushed Indra out of the heavens.

To restore balance, Vishnu took birth as Vamana, a humble dwarf Brahmin, and came to Mahabali's great sacrifice asking for only as much land as he could cover in three paces.

Bali agreed despite his guru Shukracharya's warning, and Vamana then grew to a cosmic form, covering the entire earth in one stride and the heavens in the second.

Finding no place left for the third step, the noble Bali bowed his own head, and Vishnu placed his foot upon it, sending the humbled king to rule the netherworld while blessing him for his unwavering truthfulness.

The day asks devotees to fast, keep vigil through the night, and remember that true greatness lies in keeping one's word even at the cost of everything else.
