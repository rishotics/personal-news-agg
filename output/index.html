🕉 This Day Significance & History

Bhadrapada Krishna Paksha Tritiya
Tritiya tithi, Krishna Paksha, Bhadrapada month (Shravana in the Amanta reckoning), Sunday, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today's tithi is Krishna Paksha Tritiya in the month of Bhadrapada, the third lunar day of the waning fortnight that follows last week's Shravana Purnima.

No major named festival falls on this date this year, so the day is best understood through the tithi itself, for Tritiya is traditionally kept under the guardianship of Goddess Gauri, an aspect of Parvati.

Tradition holds that the goddess, born earlier as Sati and reborn as the daughter of the mountains, undertook fierce and unwavering penance to win Lord Shiva as her husband.

Her long fast and steadfast devotion moved Shiva at last to accept her, and their union became a symbol of everlasting love and marital happiness when he took her as his consort after years of devotion.

In many households, a Tritiya such as this is marked with a simple vrat, worshipping Gauri with flowers, fruit, and sweets while abstaining from salt for the day.

What the day quietly asks of those who observe it is patience and constancy, the same qualities that carried the goddess through her long penance to its fulfillment.
