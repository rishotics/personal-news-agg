Agents

Iris: Climbing to the Search Frontier

Alibaba/independent (large-scale search agent training) · Ziyuan Liu, Hengqi Liu, Zichuan Wang, Yang Qin, Jiachen Liang
Why it matters
  1. Introduces Iris-mini and Iris-pro, search agents trained at 35B-A3B and 397B-A17B parameter scales, pushing frontier agentic search capability.
  2. Novel data pipeline reverse-constructs multi-hop search tasks directly from the hyperlink structure of a web corpus, avoiding costly manual annotation.
  3. Scaling a full training recipe (data, RL, infra) to a 397B-parameter MoE search agent is a credible, large-scale demonstration rarely published outside major labs.
  4. Matters to anyone building retrieval-augmented or deep-research agents, since search-quality bottlenecks limit most current agentic LLM systems.
  5. Signals a shift toward treating web-scale hyperlink graphs as a training signal for agentic search, a reusable recipe others will likely adopt.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Bhaum Pradosh Vrat (Krishna Paksha Trayodashi)
Krishna Paksha Trayodashi, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083 (Tuesday, hence Bhaum/Bhom Pradosh)

Today the moon stands on Krishna Trayodashi, the thirteenth day of the waning fortnight of Bhadrapada, and because it falls on a Tuesday it is kept as Bhaum or Bhom Pradosh, the fast that belongs to Lord Shiva and to Mangal, the fiery lord of the day.

Tradition explains that the twilight hour of Trayodashi, called Pradosh Kaal, is the moment Shiva is most easily pleased, and devotees keep a fast through the day and worship him and Parvati as the sun goes down.

The old katha tells of a poor widowed Brahmin woman who went begging for alms each day with her young son and returned home only in the evening, faithfully observing this same Pradosh vow despite her hardship.

One evening on the riverbank she found an injured boy, unknown to her as Dharmagupta, the prince of Vidarbha, whose father had been killed and whose kingdom had been seized by enemies.

She sheltered and raised him as her own, and in time, through the working of Shiva's grace and the widow's unbroken devotion, the prince regained his lost throne and returned to reward her and her son with a home in the palace.

The day asks householders to fast till dusk, light a single lamp before Shiva at twilight, and trust, as the widow did, that quiet faithfulness kept through hard times is eventually repaid.
