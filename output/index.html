🕉 This Day Significance & History

Bhadrapada Shukla Navami
Navami tithi, Shukla Paksha, Bhadrapada month (Vikram Samvat 2083), Purva Ashadha nakshatra

Today the moon stands on Navami, the ninth day of the bright fortnight of Bhadrapada, a tithi tradition sets apart for the worship of the Goddess.

The scriptures tell that when the buffalo-demon Mahishasura had driven the gods from heaven, the assembled devas poured out their combined radiance and from it rose Durga, blazing and unstoppable.

For nine nights she battled him across the three worlds, taking fiercer and fiercer forms as the fighting wore on.

On the ninth night, this very tithi, she finally ran him through and stood victorious over the fallen demon, restoring order to heaven and earth.

This is why Navami carries the memory of the Goddess's triumph even outside the great autumn Navratri, when it returns each month as a quieter echo of that decisive night.

The day simply asks its observers to fast, to recall her courage, and to face down whatever in their own lives plays the part of the demon.
