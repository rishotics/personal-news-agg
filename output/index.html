🕉 This Day Significance & History

Ashwina Shukla Purnima — Valmiki Jayanti
Purnima tithi, Shukla Paksha, Ashwina month (Vikram Samvat 2083)

Today the moon stands full in Ashwina, and tradition marks it as the birth day of Maharishi Valmiki, the sage revered as the Adi Kavi, the first poet of the Sanskrit language.

Before he was a sage he was Ratnakara, a highwayman who by one telling robbed travelers through the forest to feed his family.

One day he waylaid the wandering sage Narada, who asked him a simple question: would his wife and children share the sin he gathered on their behalf.

He went home and asked them, and they told him plainly that the crime was his alone to carry, not theirs.

Shaken, he returned to Narada, sat down in penance for so long that an anthill, a valmika, rose around his still body, and from it he emerged reborn as Valmiki, who would go on to compose the Ramayana and shelter Sita and her sons in his hermitage.

The day asks those who observe it to believe that no one is too stained for transformation, and that a single honest reckoning with oneself can be the start of a wholly new life.
