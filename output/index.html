🕉 This Day Significance & History

Aja Ekadashi
Krishna Paksha Ekadashi, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today is Krishna Paksha Ekadashi of Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083, known across the tradition as Aja Ekadashi.

The tale behind it belongs to King Harishchandra of Ayodhya, a ruler celebrated for his unbending devotion to truth, who according to the Brahmanda Purana lost everything after honouring a promise made to a sage.

As the legend is told, he gave away his kingdom and wealth and finally sold himself, his wife Shaivya, and his son Rohitashva, ending up as a servant at a cremation ground in Kashi, and even in that state he never abandoned dharma.

One day the sage Gautama came upon the grieving king and, moved by his suffering, instructed him to keep the fast of Aja Ekadashi with full devotion and to stay awake through the night in worship.

Harishchandra observed the vrata exactly as told, and the story holds that his sins were dissolved, his lost son was restored to life, and his kingdom and family were returned to him.

The day therefore asks its observers to fast with sincerity, keep vigil through the night in prayer to Vishnu, and above all hold to truth even when it costs everything, for the name Aja itself points to what cannot be destroyed.
