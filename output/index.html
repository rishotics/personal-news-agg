🕉 This Day Significance & History

Radha Ashtami
Shukla Ashtami, Shukla Paksha, Bhadrapada month (Vikram Samvat 2083)

Today is Radha Ashtami, the Shukla Ashtami tithi of Bhadrapada, the day Braj celebrates the appearance of Radha Rani.

Tradition holds that King Vrishabhanu and his queen Kirtida were a pious couple, yet for long years they remained childless.

The Padma Purana recounts that the king went to bathe in the Yamuna one day and felt himself surrounded by a golden radiance rising from the water.

Looking closer, he found a luminous infant girl resting on a lotus, and he carried her home to Kirtida, who raised her as their own beloved daughter.

Vaishnava tradition reveres this child as an incarnation of Lakshmi and as the eternal consort of Krishna, the very embodiment of devoted, selfless love.

On this day devotees fast, bathe her image in panchamrit, adorn her in new finery, and pray that her example might deepen their own capacity for pure devotion.
