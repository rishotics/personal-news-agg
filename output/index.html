🕉 This Day Significance & History

Lalita Panchami (Upang Lalita Vrat)
Shukla Paksha Panchami in the Ashwin lunar month, Thursday — the fifth day of Sharad Navratri

Today the moon waxes to its fifth day, Ashwin Shukla Panchami, observed by devotees as Lalita Panchami or the Upang Lalita Vrat, the fifth day of the nine-night Navratri.

Tradition tells that the demon Bhandasura, born from the ashes of the incinerated love-god Kamadeva, grew so fearsome that Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva invoked the Supreme Shakti in a great yagna to save the three worlds.

From the sacred fire rose Goddess Lalita, seated upon a lotus and armed with celestial weapons, she who is also known as Tripura Sundari and counted among the ten Mahavidyas.

Riding her chariot named Chakraraja and accompanied by the fierce Sapta Matrikas, she marched upon Bhandasura and destroyed him, restoring order and righteousness to creation.

Devotees keep the Upang Lalita Vrat today with fasting and recitation of the Lalita Sahasranama, believing that even a glimpse of the goddess can dissolve life's sorrows.

The day asks worshippers to set aside the ego and ignorance that Bhandasura embodied and to turn instead toward the grace, wisdom, and quiet strength that Lalita represents.
