Why it matters
  1. Shows that fine-tuning multimodal models on a single narrow task can cause broad harmful behavior unrelated to that task.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Shukla Paksha Chaturthi (Vinayaka Chaturthi)
Shukla Paksha, Chaturthi tithi, Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon waxes toward fullness on Chaturthi, the fourth day of Ashwin's bright fortnight, a tithi held sacred to Lord Ganesha across the Hindu year.

The tradition recalls how Goddess Parvati, wishing for a guardian loyal only to her, took the turmeric paste from her own body, shaped it into a boy, and breathed life into him, naming him Ganesha.

She set him at her door with one command, that no one should be allowed to enter while she bathed, and the boy obeyed without question.

When Lord Shiva himself returned and was refused entry by this unknown child, his fury rose and in the struggle that followed he severed the boy's head, leaving Parvati inconsolable.

To ease her grief Shiva sent his attendants to bring the head of the first living creature they found facing north, and they returned with that of a young elephant, which he placed upon the boy's body to restore him to life.

In memory of that devotion and restoration, this day asks devotees to begin new undertakings with prayer to Ganesha, the remover of obstacles, before any other god is invoked.
