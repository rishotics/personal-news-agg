🤖 Paper of the Day AI & Robotics Research

LLM

Maglev: Sliding Recurrent Memory

UT Austin / independent research · ['Bo Liu', 'Qiang Liu']
Why it matters
  1. Introduces a recurrent Transformer with fixed-size memory that generalizes sliding-window attention while staying fully parallelizable in training.
  2. Novelty lies in decoupling a full-attention prefiller from a sliding-recurrent decoder, letting long-context state compress without quadratic cost.
  3. By interleaving full and sliding-window attention layers it claims to match full-attention quality at constant memory and linear inference cost, directly tackling the KV-cache bottleneck plaguing every deployed LLM.
  4. Matters to anyone building or serving long-context LLMs, since fixed-size recurrent memory could cut serving costs dramatically versus standard transformer KV caches.
  5. If validated at scale, this architecture pattern could reshape how future foundation models balance long-context capability against inference efficiency.
