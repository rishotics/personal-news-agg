🤖 Paper of the Day AI & Robotics Research

LLM

β-OPSD: Deriving with Policy Optimization, Training with Self-Distillation

University of Maryland · Jiawei Xu, Minghui Liu, Juzheng Zhang, Tom Goldstein, Furong Huang
Why it matters
  1. Fixes on-policy self-distillation, a promising but brittle method for improving reasoning LLMs that normally needs heavy tuning to stabilize.
  2. Reveals that vanilla OPSD is just the beta=1 case of a broader family, unifying it theoretically with policy optimization and self-distillation objectives.
  3. Introduces beta as a tunable knob that lets practitioners trade off between distillation stability and policy-gradient-style optimization strength.
  4. Matters for anyone training reasoning models with self-distillation, since it turns a fragile engineering-heavy recipe into a principled, controllable method.
  5. Reframes a widely used but poorly understood training trick, giving researchers a cleaner theoretical lens to design more reliable future variants.
