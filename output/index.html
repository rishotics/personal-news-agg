🕉 This Day Significance & History

Durga Puja Begins (Maha Shashthi / Akal Bodhon)
Shukla Shashthi, Shukla Paksha, Ashwin month (Vikram Samvat 2083), Jyeshtha Nakshatra

Today is Maha Shashthi, the sixth day of Shukla Paksha in the month of Ashwin, when the five-day worship of Goddess Durga formally opens.

Tradition holds that the Goddess is properly worshipped in spring, so her autumn invocation is remembered as Akal Bodhon, the untimely awakening.

The story goes back to the Ramayana as retold by Krittivasa, when Rama found his campaign against Ravana faltering and turned to the Goddess for strength.

He set out to please her with an offering of one hundred and eight blue lotuses, but found he was one flower short and raised his bow to offer his own eye in its place.

Durga appeared before him, moved by this devotion, and granted him the power that would let him defeat Ravana and recover Sita.

The day asks devotees to rouse the same resolve in themselves, calling on the Goddess's strength before any difficult battle of their own.
