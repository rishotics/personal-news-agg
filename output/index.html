🤖 Paper of the Day AI & Robotics Research

Agents

WHALE: A Simple Recipe for Joint Harness-Weight Optimization

Stanford · ['Haechan Kim', 'Yoonho Lee', 'Gisang Lee', 'Chelsea Finn', 'Kangwook Lee']
Why it matters
  1. WHALE jointly optimizes an agent's model weights and its executable harness code instead of tuning either in isolation.
  2. The novel insight is that harness and weights bottleneck each other: better weights need a different harness and vice versa, so joint optimization breaks that deadlock.
  3. Authors led by Chelsea Finn show a simple recipe that alternates harness edits and weight updates yields consistent gains over single-axis baselines.
  4. This matters directly for the fast-growing space of coding and tool-use agents, where practitioners routinely tune prompts or scaffolds separately from model weights.
  5. It reframes agent development as a co-design problem, suggesting future agent-building pipelines should treat harness code as a first-class optimizable artifact alongside weights.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Krishna Janmashtami
Krishna Paksha Ashtami tithi, Bhadrapada month, Rohini nakshatra

Long ago in Mathura, a tyrant named Kansa locked his own sister Devaki and her husband Vasudeva in a prison cell after a heavenly voice warned him that her eighth child would bring about his end.

One after another, Kansa had killed the couple's first children the moment they were born, determined to escape the fate the prophecy had marked out for him.

On the eighth day of the dark fortnight of Bhadrapada, at midnight, under the Rohini nakshatra, Devaki gave birth to her eighth son, Krishna, inside that very cell.

As the guards fell into a strange sleep and the chains fell open on their own, Vasudeva carried the infant through a raging storm across the swollen Yamuna, the great serpent Sheshnaga spreading his hood overhead to shield the child from the rain.

When Kansa later seized the girl he found in Krishna's place, she slipped from his hands and rose into the air as the goddess Yogamaya, declaring that his destroyer was already alive and safe far away in Gokul.

On this night, devotees keep an all-night vigil, rock Krishna's cradle at the midnight hour, and break their fast only after the ritual worship is done, remembering that even in the darkest prison, deliverance can be born.
