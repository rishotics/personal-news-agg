🕉 This Day Significance & History

Shukla Shashthi — Durga Puja Bodhon (Maha Shashthi)
Shashthi tithi, Shukla Paksha, Ashwin month (Vikram Samvat 2083), Jyeshtha nakshatra

Today is the sixth day of the bright fortnight of Ashwin, the Shashthi tithi on which the Durga Puja festival formally awakens.

Tradition holds that Lord Rama, preparing to face the demon king Ravana, needed the blessings of Devi Durga but the goddess was not due to be worshipped until spring.

Unwilling to wait, Rama performed an out-of-season invocation known as the Akal Bodhan, calling the goddess forth in autumn instead of her customary season.

On this Shashthi day the Bodhon and Adhivas rituals are performed, in which the goddess is ceremonially roused from her slumber and invited into the bel tree and the newly made clay image.

Households and pandals light lamps, offer flowers, and welcome Durga as she begins her six-day sojourn among her devotees before her battle with the buffalo-demon Mahishasura is re-enacted in the coming days.

The day asks worshippers to prepare their homes and hearts with the same devotion Rama showed, inviting the mother goddess in with patience, humility and readiness for the celebrations ahead.
