🤖 Paper of the Day AI & Robotics Research

ML Theory

Massive Activations in Hybrid Linear Attention Large Language Models: Pre-Attention Spikes and Inter-Spike Plateaus

Independent/Academic (China) · Zunhai Su, Bohan Sun, Xialie Zhuang, Shuibai Zhang, He Xiao
Why it matters
  1. Presents the first systematic study of massive activations specifically inside hybrid linear-attention LLMs, a rapidly growing architecture class.
  2. Discovers two novel, architecture-aligned phenomena: pre-attention spikes that consistently occur right before full attention layers, and inter-spike plateaus that persist through linear attention layers.
  3. This mechanistic finding is directly credible because it is empirically tied to layer position across multiple hybrid models, not a single anecdotal case.
  4. Matters to anyone building or quantizing hybrid Mamba/attention hybrids, since massive activations are known to wreck low-bit quantization and outlier handling.
  5. Changes how engineers should design quantization, KV-cache compression, and pruning schemes for hybrid linear attention models by targeting these newly identified spike locations.
