🕉 This Day Significance & History

Valmiki Jayanti (Ashwin Shukla Purnima)
Purnima tithi, Shukla Paksha, Ashwin month (Vikram Samvat 2083), Ashwini nakshatra

Today, the full moon of Ashwin, Hindus mark the birth anniversary of Maharishi Valmiki, revered as the Adi Kavi, the first poet of Sanskrit.

Tradition holds that before he became a sage, Valmiki was known as Ratnakar, a highway robber who attacked travellers to provide for his family.

His life changed when he encountered the divine sage Narada, who asked him whether his family would share the karmic burden of his violent deeds, and Ratnakar realised they would not.

Shaken by this truth, he turned to penance at Narada's guidance, meditating so long and so still that an anthill, a valmika, grew up around his body, and when he emerged he was named Valmiki.

It was this transformed sage who later sheltered Sita in his forest hermitage and taught the story of Rama to her twin sons Lava and Kusha, thereby becoming the author and first narrator of the Ramayana.

The day asks devotees to remember that redemption is always possible, and that even a life steeped in wrongdoing can be remade through remorse, guidance, and disciplined devotion.
