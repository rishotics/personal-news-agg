🤖 Paper of the Day AI & Robotics Research

LLM

Knowledge Distillation During Mid-Training Favors Reasoning over Factual Recall

University of Washington / Meta · Jacqueline He, Howard Yen, Shuyue Stella Li, Margaret Li, Hanqing Zeng
Why it matters
  1. Shows forward-KL logit distillation during mid-training systematically boosts reasoning skills while suppressing factual recall in student LMs.
  2. Novel finding: the training-stage location of distillation, not just teacher strength, determines what capabilities transfer to the student.
  3. Controlled experiments isolate the effect across pretraining, mid-training, and post-training stages, giving credible causal evidence rather than anecdotal benchmarks.
  4. Directly relevant to every lab building smaller efficient models via distillation from frontier teachers, including Meta, Google, and Alibaba's Qwen line.
  5. Suggests practitioners should rethink when to apply distillation depending on whether they prioritize reasoning benchmarks or knowledge-intensive QA performance.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Krishna Paksha Saptami
Krishna Paksha Saptami tithi, Bhadrapada month, Krittika nakshatra, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon stands on Krishna Paksha Saptami, the seventh day of the waning fortnight in the month of Bhadrapada, under Krittika nakshatra.

Tradition holds that every Saptami belongs to Surya Dev, the sun god who crosses the sky each day on a chariot drawn by seven horses.

The story behind this bond goes back to Samba, the son of Lord Krishna, who was struck with a disfiguring skin affliction after a moment of youthful arrogance.

Sages who saw his suffering counselled him to turn to the sun itself, urging fasting and prayer offered with full devotion on Saptami.

Year after year he persisted, and tradition says his affliction slowly lifted as Surya's light burned away the curse.

The day therefore asks those who observe it to rise before sunrise, offer water to the sun, and seek from Surya the gifts of health, clarity and steady resolve.
