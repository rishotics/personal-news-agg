Scaling Properties of Text Conditioning in Visual Generation

ByteDance · ['Zilong Chen', 'Chaorui Deng', 'Kunchang Li', 'Hongyi Yuan', 'Haoqi Fan']
Why it matters
  1. Establishes the first empirical scaling law for how text conditioning affects diffusion-based visual generation quality.
  2. Novel finding: converged diffusion loss scales with the amount of semantic information in prompts, not just token count, unlike prior scaling work.
  3. Overturns the assumption that diffusion loss curves are uninformative for text conditioning, giving a measurable predictive framework instead.
  4. Directly relevant to every lab building text-to-image and text-to-video systems, since prompt engineering and encoder choice have lacked principled guidance.
  5. Opens a path to designing better text encoders and prompt strategies by predicting gains before costly full-scale training runs.
