🕉 This Day Significance & History

Varaha Jayanti (Bhadrapada Shukla Dwitiya)
Shukla Paksha Dwitiya (Dvitiya) tithi, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083, Hasta Nakshatra, Sunday

Tradition holds that Bhadrapada Shukla Dwitiya is Varaha Jayanti, the day Lord Vishnu descended to earth as the mighty boar Varaha.

The story goes that the demon Hiranyaksha had grown arrogant with power and dragged Bhudevi, the Earth herself, down into the depths of the cosmic ocean, throwing all of creation into peril.

To restore balance, Vishnu took the form of Varaha, plunged into the waters, and after a fierce, prolonged battle slew Hiranyaksha.

Then, in the most tender part of the tale, Varaha lifted the drowning Earth upon his tusks and carried her gently back to her rightful place above the waters.

In temples such as Tirumala, Srimushnam, and Ahobilam, devotees mark the day with pujas, abhishekams, and readings from the Varaha Purana.

The day asks those who observe it to remember that whenever the world sinks under the weight of greed or arrogance, righteousness will find a way to lift it back up.
