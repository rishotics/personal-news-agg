Learning to Solve Hard Problems in RL for LLMs by Never Giving Up

Allen Institute for AI / Mila · ['Michael Noukhovitch', 'Hamish Ivison', 'Nathan Lambert', 'Aaron Courville']
Why it matters
  1. Identifies a Matthew Effect where RL post-training mostly sharpens what LLMs already solve rather than fixing genuinely hard problems.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Skanda Shashthi (Bhadrapada Shukla Shashthi)
Shukla Paksha Panchami gives way to Shashthi tithi around mid-morning, in the Hindu month of Bhadrapada, Vikram Samvat 2083, under Vishakha nakshatra.

At sunrise the tithi is still Panchami, but within a couple of hours it turns into Shashthi, and tradition holds that this very meeting point of the fifth and sixth day is the one preferred for the Skanda Shashti vrat of Lord Muruga.

The tale behind the day begins with a demon named Soorapadman who, with his brothers Simhamukhan and Tarakasuran, overran the Devas and made himself master of the three worlds.

Unable to bear his tyranny any longer, the gods went to Shiva for help, and it was his young son Skanda, born to lead the divine armies, who took up the fight.

For six days Skanda battled Soorapadman, and on the sixth he hurled his Vel and split the demon in two, one half rising as the peacock that became his mount, the other as the rooster on his banner.

Devotees mark this Shashthi with the Kanda Sashti Kavacham and with fasting through the day, treating the Vel's victory as a reminder that cruelty does not have the last word.

What the day asks of those who observe it is restraint and remembrance: keep the fast, chant Skanda's name, and carry a little of his courage into one's own struggles against fear and wrongdoing.
