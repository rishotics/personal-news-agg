🕉 This Day Significance & History

Vishwakarma Puja (Shukla Paksha Shashthi)
Shukla Paksha Shashthi, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083 (Nakshatra: Anuradha)

Today the moon stands on Shukla Paksha Shashthi of Bhadrapada, and in workshops and factories across India the day is kept as Vishwakarma Puja.

Vishwakarma is remembered as the divine architect and celestial engineer, the craftsman god whose creations include the city of Lanka and the flying vimana called Pushpaka, the cities of Indraprastha and Dwarka, and even Shiva's trident and Vishnu's discus.

One tale tells that when Shiva married Parvati, he asked Vishwakarma to raise them a palace, and the result was a dazzling home built entirely of gold.

At the housewarming, Shiva invited the sage-king Ravana to perform the rites, and when Ravana, awestruck, asked for the golden palace itself as his fee, Shiva could not refuse; thus Golden Lanka became Ravana's own.

Vishwakarma is also credited with raising Dwarka, the sea-girt capital where Krishna later ruled, a city still honoured today as a place of pilgrimage.

The day asks craftsmen, engineers and labourers to set down their tools, machines and instruments before the divine builder, seeking his blessing for skill, safety and honest work in the year ahead.
