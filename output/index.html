🕉 This Day Significance & History

Kajari Teej
Krishna Paksha Tritiya, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the tithi at sunrise is Krishna Paksha Tritiya of the Bhadrapada month, and it is kept as Kajari Teej, known in different regions as Kajali Teej, Kajri Teej, Badi Teej and Satudi Teej.

The festival is built around the devotion of Goddess Parvati, who is worshipped alongside Lord Shiva by women praying for love, harmony and well-being in married life.

In many homes a neem branch is set up and honoured as Neemdi Mata, a form of Parvati, and women offer her roli, akshat, flowers, mehendi and sindoor before worshipping her together with Shiva.

A well-loved katha tells of a poor Brahmin whose devoted wife longed to observe the vrat but had no sattu for the puja, so at night he took flour, ghee and sugar from a shopkeeper to help her complete it.

Caught in the act, the Brahmin explained he had taken the ingredients only so his wife could finish her Teej worship, and the shopkeeper, moved by their devotion, forgave him and gave him everything needed to complete the puja.

The day asks married women to fast from sunrise, remember that devotion and honesty move even a stranger's heart, and break their vrat only after sighting the moon and offering it arghya.
