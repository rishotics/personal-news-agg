🕉 This Day Significance & History

Shukla Paksha Pratipada
Shukla Paksha Pratipada tithi (till 07:46 AM, then Dwitiya), Bhadrapada month begins today under the Amanta system, Uttara Phalguni Nakshatra till 12:55 PM, Saturday

Today is Pratipada, the very first tithi of the bright fortnight, the moment the moon reappears in the sky after vanishing completely on Amavasya.

Tradition traces this monthly rebirth to Chandra, the moon god, who married the twenty-seven star-daughters of the sage Daksha but showered all his love on one wife, Rohini, while neglecting the rest.

The other wives went weeping to their father, and Daksha, feeling disrespected, cursed Chandra to waste away with a consuming disease until his light failed entirely.

Grief-stricken, Chandra travelled to Prabhasa on the western shore, shaped a lingam of earth with his own hands, and worshipped Shiva through six months of unbroken penance.

Moved by his devotion, Shiva could not undo Daksha's word but softened it, decreeing that Chandra would wane for a fortnight and then wax for the next, so that today marks the day his brightening begins anew.

The day asks those who observe it to remember that decline is never final, and that humility and sincere devotion, like Chandra's, can restore what pride and favouritism once took away.
