LimiX team (industry/academia collaboration) · ['Xingxuan Zhang', 'Gang Ren', 'Hao Yuan', 'Hao Zou', 'Hongze Tan']
Why it matters
  1. LimiX-2 introduces Contextual Mechanism Networks trained via Context-Conditional Masked Modeling, a new paradigm for structured-data intelligence beyond text.
  2. It targets tabular and structured data, a domain long dominated by gradient-boosted trees rather than large pretrained foundation models.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Vishwakarma Puja (Vishwakarma Jayanti)
Shukla Paksha Shashthi, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083 (coinciding with Kanya Sankranti)

Today the Hindu calendar marks Shukla Paksha Shashthi in the month of Bhadrapada, and it is on this Kanya Sankranti that devotees observe Vishwakarma Puja.

Tradition holds that Vishwakarma, son of Brahma, is the divine architect of the cosmos, credited across the four yugas with raising the heavenly Swarg Loke for Indra, the golden citadel of Lanka, the sea-girt city of Dwarka for Krishna, and Indraprastha for the Pandavas.

The most retold of his legends says that when Shiva wed Parvati, he asked Vishwakarma to build them a palace, and the craftsman fashioned it entirely of gold, only for the visiting sage-king Ravana to demand the dazzling home as a gift after performing its house-warming rites, which is how the Golden Lanka became his own.

Another cherished account tells of Vishwakarma appearing before King Indradyumna as a mysterious old carpenter who agreed to carve the sacred images of Jagannath, Balabhadra, and Subhadra on the condition that he be left undisturbed, work that was left with unfinished hands and feet after the impatient king peeked too soon.

On this day workshops, factories, and engineering units across India fall quiet from routine labour as tools, machines, and instruments are washed, garlanded, and set before the god's image for blessing, a practice that turns shop floors into sites of festivity.

What the day asks of those who observe it is simple: to pause and honour the instruments of one's craft, for skill without gratitude, tradition suggests, builds nothing that truly lasts.
