Academic/robotics research collaboration · ['Jiaming Zhou', 'Qihang Zhang', 'Gangwei Xu', 'Cunxin Fan', 'Yujie Zhao']
Why it matters
  1. Tackles zero-shot cross-task generalization in robot manipulation, a long-standing bottleneck for deployable robot policies.
  2. Introduces in-context world-action modeling learned directly from human videos, avoiding costly robot-specific demonstration collection.
  3. Borrows the in-context learning paradigm that made LLMs task-flexible and applies it to embodied action prediction, a genuinely novel transfer.

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Raksha Bandhan (Shravana Purnima)
Shukla Purnima, Shukla Paksha, Shravana month, Vikram Samvat 2083

The Hindu calendar places today on the full moon of Shravana, the Purnima tithi that closes the bright fortnight of this rain-soaked month.

Tradition holds that during a fierce moment of battle Lord Krishna's finger was cut by his own discus, and Draupadi tore a strip from the end of her saree to bind the wound and stop the bleeding.

Krishna is said to have told her that he now owed her a debt, one he would repay whenever she called upon him.

That debt came due years later in the Kaurava court, when Draupadi was dragged before the assembly and Dushasana tried to strip her of her garment, and Krishna miraculously kept her saree endless so her honour could never be stripped away.

This bond of a thread given and a protection promised is the thread of memory that Hindus invoke today, as sisters tie rakhi on their brothers' wrists and brothers vow to shield them through every season of life.

The day asks people to renew that old covenant of care, offering sweets, prayers, and a promise that protection between kin is a debt sacred enough to outlast even a king's court.
