🕉 This Day Significance & History

Bhadrapada Shukla Navami
On Sunday, 20 September 2026, the tithi at sunrise is Shukla Navami in the Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083. This tithi will remain until 05:51 PM, after which Shukla Paksha Dasami Tithi will begin.

Today the moon stands on Navami, the ninth tithi of the bright fortnight of Bhadrapada, a day tradition has long linked to the Mother Goddess in her fiercest, most complete form.

The old story tells of the buffalo-demon Mahishasura, who had won a boon that no man or god could slay him, and so turned his army loose upon the three worlds.

In answer, the assembled gods poured their combined radiance into a single being, Durga, arming her with a trident from Shiva, a discus from Vishnu, and a thunderbolt from Indra.

For nine days and nights she fought his shape-shifting hordes, and it is the ninth day that tradition remembers as the moment her power reached its final, unstoppable pitch just before the demon fell.

Because of this, Navami through the year carries an echo of that day, kept by many as a time to light a lamp for the Devi, offer red flowers, and seek her courage against one's own fears and obstacles.

What the day quietly asks of the devotee is to meet whatever 'Mahishasura' troubles their own life, be it anger, doubt, or despair, with the same steady resolve the goddess showed to the very end.
