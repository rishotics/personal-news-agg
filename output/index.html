Shanghai AI Laboratory · Kai Chen, Jifeng Ding, Ning Ding, Jiaye Ge, Lixin Gu
Why it matters
  1. Introduces Mobius-v0, a new architecture that decouples knowledge storage from reasoning via a shared global Memory FFN and multiple Reasoner Self-Attn modules.
