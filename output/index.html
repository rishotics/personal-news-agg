🕉 This Day Significance & History

Rishi Panchami
Shukla Paksha Panchami, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083 (Tuesday)

Today falls on Shukla Paksha Panchami of Bhadrapada, the tithi kept across the Hindu calendar as Rishi Panchami in honour of the Saptarishis, the seven great sages of Vedic tradition.

Tradition holds that in the land of Vidarbha there lived a learned Brahmin named Uttank, whose devoted wife had borne him a son and a widowed daughter.

One night the daughter's body was found covered with worms, and through his spiritual insight Uttank discovered that in a previous life she had entered the kitchen and touched sacred vessels during her period of monthly impurity, and had also neglected this very vrat.

Grief-stricken at her suffering, the father told her that only the Rishi Panchami fast, offered to the seven sages with full devotion, could wash away so old a lapse.

She bathed, fasted, worshipped Kashyapa, Atri, Bharadwaja, Vishwamitra, Gautama, Jamadagni and Vashishtha, and listened to their sacred story, and as the puja concluded the worms vanished and her body was healed.

The day therefore asks the faithful, especially women, to seek the sages' forgiveness for unwitting lapses in ritual purity and to renew their reverence for the wisdom the Saptarishis embody.
