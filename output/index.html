🕉 This Day Significance & History

Shukra Pradosh Vrat (Ashwin, Shukla Trayodashi)
Shukla Paksha, Dwadashi tithi till afternoon giving way to Trayodashi, Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon is waxing through Shukla Paksha in the month of Ashwin, with Dwadashi tithi yielding to Trayodashi by afternoon, and because that Trayodashi falls on a Friday the evening twilight is kept as Shukra Pradosh Vrat.

Tradition recalls that Chandra, the moon god, married the twenty-seven daughters of Daksha Prajapati but lavished all his affection on Rohini while neglecting the rest, and when Daksha urged fairness and was ignored he cursed Chandra with a wasting disease.

As the moon began to shrink and the world fell toward darkness, the alarmed gods turned to Brahma, who advised Chandra to worship Shiva in the form of a lingam, and Chandra performed penance for six months.

Pleased with this devotion, Shiva appeared as a Jyotirlinga and softened the curse, decreeing that Chandra would wane for a fortnight and wax again for another, which is why the moon still grows and fades in cycles today.

It is said this blessing was granted on a Trayodashi during the pradosh kaal, the twilight window just before nightfall, and that is why this hour is still held to be the most potent time to seek Shiva's grace.

Because this particular Trayodashi falls on a Friday, the day of Shukra, devotees fast, bathe at dusk, and pray to Shiva and Parvati together for health, peace, and harmony in their homes.
