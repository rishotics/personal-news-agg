🕉 This Day Significance & History

Kartika Krishna Tritiya
Krishna Paksha, Tritiya tithi, Kartika month (purnimanta)/Ashwin (amanta), Vikram Samvat 2083

Today falls in the dark fortnight of Kartika, the month the scriptures call dearest to Lord Vishnu, who is worshipped through it in his infant form as Damodara.

The name Damodara recalls young Krishna in Vrindavan, who stole butter from the churns of the gopis and was finally caught red-handed by his mother Yashoda.

Exasperated, Yashoda tried to bind the child's waist with a rope, but no matter how many lengths she joined, the cord always fell two fingers short.

Only when the mischievous god allowed his mother's love to overpower his own divine power did the rope finally hold, binding his belly and giving him the name Damodara, the one bound by a cord.

Through Kartika, devotees retell this story each evening and light small lamps in homes and temples as an offering to this bound, beloved form of Krishna.

The day asks only quiet devotion: a lamp lit at dusk, the Damodara story remembered, and a reminder that even the almighty surrenders to love freely given.
