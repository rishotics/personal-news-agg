🕉 This Day Significance & History

Teja Dashami (Bhadrapada Shukla Dashami)
Shukla Paksha Dashami (giving way to Ekadashi by evening), Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon sits on the tenth tithi of the bright fortnight of Bhadrapada, a day the almanacs of Rajasthan mark as Teja Dashami.

The day belongs to Veer Tejaji, a folk hero still worshipped across rural Rajasthan and neighbouring states, whom tradition holds was born to Dhaluya Jat parents in the village of Kharnal in the mid-thirteenth century.

Storytellers say the young Tejaji once gave his solemn word to a serpent that he would return to it once his task was done, and that he would not break that promise even at the cost of his own life.

He rode out to recover a herd of cows that had been driven off by Mer raiders, and tradition recounts that he fought so fiercely to bring the cattle home that his body was cut and wounded almost everywhere.

Bound by the vow he had given, he is said to have offered himself to the serpent regardless, and he died in that encounter, remembered ever after as a martyr who gave his life defending cattle and keeping his word.

The day asks devotees to hold fast to a promise once made and to honour the protection of cows and the weak, and fairs are still held in his memory across the villages that revere him.
