ML Theory

The Lattice of Transition Laws

University of Pennsylvania / Apple (Jiatao Gu) · ['T. Y. Tsui', 'Jiatao Gu', 'Lingjie Liu']
Why it matters
  1. Proposes a unifying theoretical framework, the lattice of transition laws, that places diffusion and autoregressive generation on a single spectrum rather than treating them as separate paradigms
  2. Shows that existing hybrid diffusion-AR models are special fixed-schedule cases within a much larger, previously unexplored design space of decoding orders
  3. Offers a principled way to derive new generative model variants by choosing different transition laws, rather than hand-designing hybrids heuristically
  4. Matters to anyone building next-generation generative models for text, images, or multimodal data, since diffusion-AR hybrids are an active frontier at major labs
  5. Could reshape how researchers design future generative architectures by giving them a systematic map instead of ad hoc hybridization tricks

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Navratri Day 2 — Shukla Paksha Dwitiya, Maa Brahmacharini
Shukla Paksha Dwitiya (Bij) of the Ashwin month, Monday, Swati Nakshatra, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today is the second day of Sharad Navratri, when the Hindu month of Ashwin is in its bright Shukla fortnight and the tithi is Dwitiya.

Tradition holds that this day belongs to Maa Brahmacharini, the second of the nine forms of Durga worshipped during the nine nights.

She is said to be Parvati, daughter of the Himalaya, who left the comforts of her father's palace to win Lord Shiva as her husband.

The story tells that she survived first on fruit, then only on leaves, and finally on nothing at all, earning her the name Aparna, as her tapasya deepened over long years.

Her unwavering penance is remembered as the triumph of patience and self-discipline over every hardship placed in her path.

The day asks devotees to sit with that same steadiness, offering simple prayers and renewed resolve rather than haste or complaint.
