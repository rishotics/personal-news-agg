🕉 This Day Significance & History

Krishna Paksha Panchami (Shraddha Tithi in Pitru Paksha)
Krishna Paksha Panchami, Ashwin month, Vikram Samvat 2083, Rohini Nakshatra

Today the moon wanes through its fifth day, Panchami, in the dark fortnight of Ashwin, and this entire fortnight is kept as Pitru Paksha, the time set aside for the ancestors.

Tradition traces the origin of this observance to Karna, the great warrior of the Mahabharata, who gave away gold and riches all his life but never once offered food or water to his forefathers.

When Karna died and reached the heavens, he was served only gold and jewels at his table, leaving him hungry and bewildered before the gods.

Indra, king of the heavens, explained that Karna had fed the needy with wealth but had never performed shraddha for his own ancestors, so only wealth returned to him in the afterlife.

Moved by this, Indra sent Karna back to earth for fifteen days to offer food, water, and prayers to his ancestors, and this period became fixed as Pitru Paksha.

The day asks households to remember those who came before them, offering water, rice, and sesame with gratitude so that the ancestors may rest in peace and bless the living.
