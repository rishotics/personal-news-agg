🕉 This Day Significance & History

Vamana Jayanti (Bhadrapada Shukla Dwadashi)
Dwadashi tithi, Shukla Paksha, Bhadrapada month (Shravana nakshatra prevailing till mid-morning), Vikram Samvat 2083

Today falls on the Dwadashi tithi of Bhadrapada's bright fortnight, the day tradition marks as Vamana Jayanti, the appearance day of Lord Vishnu's dwarf-Brahmin avatar.

The tale begins with King Bali, grandson of the devout Prahlada, who through great merit and generosity had conquered the three worlds and displaced Indra from the heavens.

To restrain the proud king without destroying his virtue, Vishnu took birth as Vamana, a small Brahmin boy, and arrived at Bali's sacrificial grounds asking only for as much land as he could cover in three paces.

Bali agreed despite his guru Shukracharya's warning that the dwarf was no ordinary mendicant, and Vamana then grew to cosmic size, spanning the earth in one stride and the heavens in the next.

Finding no place left for the third step, Bali humbly offered his own head, and the Lord's foot upon it sent the king down to rule the netherworld, Patala, while granting him a yearly return to visit his people.

The day asks devotees to fast, worship Vishnu, and reflect on Bali's lesson, that true greatness lies in surrendering pride and keeping one's word even at great personal cost.
