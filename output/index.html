🕉 This Day Significance & History

Pradosh Vrat (Shukla Trayodashi)
Trayodashi tithi, Shukla Paksha, Bhadrapada month, Vikram Samvat 2083

Today the moon waxes toward fullness on Trayodashi, the thirteenth day of Bhadrapada's bright fortnight, and the sacred twilight hour called Pradosh Kaal belongs to Lord Shiva.

Tradition tells that Chandra, the moon god, once married the twenty-seven daughters of Daksha Prajapati but showered his love only on Rohini, neglecting the rest.

The wounded Daksha cursed Chandra to lose his radiance and waste away entirely, plunging the world into darkness.

In desperation Chandra sought refuge at a Shiva temple and performed penance through the twilight of Trayodashi, pouring out prayers to the great ascetic god.

Moved by this devotion, Shiva could not undo Daksha's curse but softened it, granting Chandra a cycle of waning and waxing so his light would always return.

Since that day, devotees observe the Pradosh fast and pray to Shiva and Parvati in the evening twilight, seeking release from suffering and the restoration of what has dimmed in their own lives.
