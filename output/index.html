🤖 Paper of the Day AI & Robotics Research

LLM

Knowledge Distillation During Mid-Training Favors Reasoning over Factual Recall

University of Washington / Meta (collaboration) · Jacqueline He, Howard Yen, Shuyue Stella Li, Margaret Li, Hanqing Zeng
Why it matters
  1. Runs controlled experiments isolating how forward-KL knowledge distillation affects LMs differently across pretraining stages rather than just final performance
  2. Finds a genuinely new and counterintuitive split: mid-training distillation boosts reasoning ability but actively hurts factual recall retention
  3. Result is credible because it uses systematic ablations comparing teacher-student pairs across multiple training checkpoints and task types
  4. Directly affects anyone building smaller distilled LLMs for production, since blind distillation could silently degrade factual knowledge
  5. Changes best practices going forward by suggesting distillation curricula should be stage-aware, mixing KD with other objectives depending on target capability

🕉 This Day Significance & History

Shitala Satam (Sheetala Saptami)
Krishna Paksha Saptami, Shravana month in the Gujarati amanta reckoning (Bhadrapada in the North Indian purnimanta calendar), Vikram Samvat 2083

Today falls on Krishna Paksha Saptami, the seventh day of the dark fortnight, observed in Gujarat and neighbouring regions as Shitala Satam, the day after Randhan Chhath.

Tradition holds that on the night of Randhan Chhath the cooling goddess Shitala Ma visits every home to bless the family hearth.

In one household a weary daughter-in-law fell asleep without dousing the embers of the stove, and when the goddess touched it she was badly burned.

Enraged at being scorched by the very fire that should have been put out in her honour, Shitala Ma cursed the young woman that her own son would be consumed as she had been, and by morning the child lay burned to death.

Ever since, Gujarati households light no fire and cook no fresh food on Shitala Satam, eating instead the meal prepared the previous evening so the hearth stays cold and undisturbed.

The day asks devotees, mothers especially, to worship Shitala Ma for protection from fevers and pox, and to remember that even small carelessness with fire can bring grief to a whole household.
