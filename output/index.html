Vision

Invisible Shortcuts: Why Vision Encoders Know Your Camera

Other (academic, likely CTU Prague/CVUT & collaborators) · Vladan Stojnić, Ryan Ramos, Giorgos Kordopatis-Zilos, Noa Garcia, Giorgos Tolias
Why it matters
  1. Reveals that vision encoders exploit invisible pixel-level metadata traces, not just visible texture or background shortcuts, to solve tasks.
  2. Introduces a new category of shortcut learning distinct from prior visible-bias research, expanding how we think about dataset contamination.
  3. Demonstrates the effect is strong enough that models can infer camera identity purely from encoded pixel artifacts, a striking and testable claim.
  4. Affects anyone training or evaluating vision or multimodal models on web-scraped or camera-diverse datasets, since benchmarks may be silently compromised.
  5. Forces a rethink of evaluation protocols and dataset curation practices across the vision and multimodal research community going forward.
